Drives a <video> from a capture→media time mapping, so the app owns the timeline.

The media option normally assumes video time = capture time. Here the video is scrubbed from our own timeline via { element, offset, rate } or a time(t) callback — CCapture still does the frame-accurate seek. Records a WebM.

Note: frame-stepped <video> capture depends on the browser decoding paused seeks. On some GPUs the video frame comes out black (the overlay, driven by the mapped time, still shows the mapping working). See the README for details.
